Blindfire: Lights Out

An intense online multiplayer shooter where the lights are off and the stakes are high. Rely on sound and high-tech gadgets to survive the pitch-black arenas. Free to Play on Steam.

The Pitch Black Awaits

Set in a neon-drenched underworld, Blindfire pits contestants against each other in illicit firefights where every shot fired is a gamble.

🌑

Light Is Your Enemy

In pitch-black close-quarter arenas, every muzzle flash risks exposing your position to the pack. Master the Echo mechanic to navigate and hunt opponents without giving yourself away.

🔫

Master the Arsenal

Choose from 9 different weapons tailored for stealth, power or precision. Pistols, shotguns, and precision rifles — each with unique handling in the dark.

🎮

Audio Aim Assist

Built to ensure more players can compete effectively in the dark. Clear audio cues help you stay oriented and find your targets regardless of your level of vision.

💀

Death Is Not the End

Eliminated players gain access to night vision cameras and can control environmental traps — from the Jack in the Box to the Confetti Cannon — to mess with survivors.

🎯

Tactical Gadgets

Use your tools and environmental traps to outmaneuver and outthink your opponents. Shadow your way to victory with strategy as sharp as your aim.

🎨

Deep Customization

Unlock a massive range of blacklight skins and masks as you climb through the 50-level progression system. Make your mark in the neon-drenched underworld.
